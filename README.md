## Hi, I'm Victor Li!

I'm a student at Northeastern University pursuing a B.S. in Artificial Intelligence with minors in Mathematics and Physics, currently on co-op as an AI/ML Engineer at the CMS Experiment @ CERN. I like building things that mix code, math, and creativity — from LLM/RAG systems and physics data pipelines to full-stack apps and electronics projects. Outside of tech, I practice sewing, jewelry making, and making art, which shapes how I think about design and problem-solving.

## Interests
- **LLM/RAG Engineering** - building retrieval-augmented systems with real evaluation rigor: ablation studies, hallucination detection, ground-truth benchmarks.
- **Applied ML for Scientific Data** - applying machine learning to physics, materials science, and experimental instrument data.
- **Autonomous Systems** - designing systems that integrate perception, control, and planning in real-world environments.
- **AI Security** - studying the reliability, robustness, and adversarial failure modes of intelligent systems.

## Selected Projects
- [HEP-RAG](https://github.com/vvictorrr/HEP-RAG) - citation-grounded Q&A over HEP physics papers, with dual-signal (LLM + NLI) hallucination detection.
- [DnD Encounter Planner](https://github.com/vvictorrr/DnD-Encounter-Planner) - full-stack D&D 5e encounter planner simulating a whole adventuring day of HP and resource attrition.
- [Battery Prognostics](https://github.com/vvictorrr/Battery-Prognostics-Analysis) - ML models for lithium-ion battery capacity fade and remaining useful life prediction.
- [Debt Tracker](https://github.com/vvictorrr/debt-tracker) - app to track and settle shared debts between friends.
- [Blackjack Algorithm](https://github.com/vvictorrr/Blackjack-Odds) - optimal strategy calculator using dynamic programming and probability theory.
